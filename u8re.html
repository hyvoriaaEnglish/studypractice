🌍 TOURISM – READING CLOZE

B1–B2 Level | Reading Cloze / Multiple-choice Cloze

In recent years, tourism has become one of the fastest-growing industries in many countries. While international travel remains popular, (1) ______ tourism has also attracted a large number of visitors who prefer exploring their own country. Travelling locally is often more affordable and allows people to discover places they may have previously overlooked.

When planning a holiday, many tourists choose to contact a travel (2) ______, who can recommend suitable destinations and arrange accommodation and transportation. Others prefer to use an (3) ______ to book flights, hotels and activities themselves. Before setting off, experienced travellers usually prepare a detailed (4) ______ so that they know exactly where they will go and what they will do each day.

For those who want to experience local culture, staying in a (5) ______ can be an excellent choice. Instead of staying in an expensive hotel, visitors live with a local family and have the opportunity to learn about their customs and daily lives. Food tourism is another growing trend, with travellers visiting different regions mainly to try local specialities and traditional dishes.

The time of year can also have a significant effect on the cost of a holiday. Travelling during the (6) ______ is generally cheaper because hotels and airlines often offer lower prices when there are fewer visitors. In contrast, prices can become extremely high during peak periods.

In large cities, tourists often take a (7) ______ bus, which allows them to get off at popular attractions and get back on whenever they wish. Those who prefer greater independence may choose a (8) ______ tour, using maps or audio guides instead of travelling with a professional guide.

However, tourism is not always beneficial. Popular destinations can become overcrowded, and the environment may suffer as a result. For this reason, sustainable tourism is becoming increasingly important. Tourists are encouraged to respect local communities, protect natural areas and avoid activities that may cause (9) ______ damage to the environment.

Ultimately, a successful holiday does not always require travelling to a (10) ______ destination. Sometimes, the most memorable experiences can be found by simply wandering through a small town, trying local food or talking to the people who live there.
