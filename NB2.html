Question 1
A recent survey of a language learning magazine has consulted a number of experts in the ______ of a second language acquisition.
A. branch
B. field
C. section
D. part
Correct answer: B. field

Explanation: "The field of..." is a common collocation meaning a particular area or subject of study or research. Here, "the field of second language acquisition" refers to the academic area concerned with how people learn a second language.
Question 2
Their advice may prove ______ for those considering a language course.
A. invaluable
B. valueless
C. worthless
D. invalidate
Correct answer: A. invaluable

Explanation: "Invaluable" means extremely useful or valuable. The experts' advice is very useful for people considering taking a language course.

Note that "invaluable" does NOT mean "without value". It means "extremely valuable".
Question 3
One suggestion is that you ______ whether you are likely to be successful at learning a language.
A. survey
B. review
C. balance
D. assess
Correct answer: D. assess

Explanation: "Assess" means to evaluate or judge something carefully. The sentence advises learners to evaluate whether they are likely to succeed at learning a language.
Question 4
The major ______ will be your own time and effort.
A. price
B. charge
C. cost
D. valuation
Correct answer: C. cost

Explanation: "Cost" refers to what is required or sacrificed in order to achieve something. In this context, the major cost of learning a language is your own time and effort.
Question 5

Explanation: "Recognized qualifications" are qualifications that are officially accepted or approved by institutions or employers. The natural collocation is: recognized qualifications.
