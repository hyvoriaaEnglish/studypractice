PART 1 — Multiple Choice

Question 26
We all have to follow the rules - none of us is ______ the law.
A. beyond
B. over
C. above
D. onto
Correct answer: C. above

Explanation: The fixed expression "above the law" means not subject to the law or not required to obey it. The sentence says that everyone must follow the rules, so nobody is above the law.
Question 27
The defendant answered every question from the judge ______ he had no prior knowledge of the incident whatsoever.
A. in that
B. as if
C. as such
D. in case
Correct answer: B. as if

Explanation: "As if" introduces a clause describing how something appears or seems to be. The defendant answered the questions as if he had no prior knowledge of the incident.
Question 28
By the time I reached the station, the last train had already departed. I ______ in such a panic after all.
A. didn’t need to leave
B. mustn’t have left
C. couldn’t have left
D. needn’t have left
Correct answer: D. needn’t have left

Explanation: "Needn't have + past participle" means something was done but it was unnecessary. The speaker left in a panic, but the last train had already departed, so leaving in such a panic was unnecessary.
Question 29
Widely regarded as one of the most innovative architects of the twentieth century, ______.
A. critics have long admired the buildings of Le Corbusier
B. it was Le Corbusier’s buildings that inspired a whole generation
C. Le Corbusier’s influence can still be felt in modern urban design
D. Le Corbusier left an indelible mark on the history of architecture
Correct answer: D. Le Corbusier left an indelible mark on the history of architecture

Explanation: The introductory participial phrase "Widely regarded..." must logically modify the subject immediately following it. Only option D has Le Corbusier as the subject, so the sentence is grammatically correct.
Question 30
After the scandal broke, the politician’s reputation was so ______ damaged that even his closest allies began to distance themselves from him.
A. inadvertently
B. irreparably
C. inherently
D. intrusively
Correct answer: B. irreparably

Explanation: "Irreparably" means in a way that cannot be repaired, corrected, or restored. "An irreparably damaged reputation" means a reputation that has been damaged beyond repair.
Question 31
The ethics committee has demanded that all submitted research data ______ by an independent body before publication.
A. has been verified
B. is verified
C. be verified
D. was verified
Correct answer: C. be verified

Explanation: After verbs such as demand, suggest, insist, recommend, formal English can use the subjunctive: demand that + subject + base verb Because the sentence is passive: demand that the data be verified.
Question 32
She always intended to start meditating but never ______ it because of her daily commitments.
A. went in for
B. got round to
C. came down with
D. looked out for
Correct answer: B. got round to

Explanation: "Get round to doing something" means to finally find time to do something that you have intended to do. She intended to start meditating but never got round to it because she was busy.
Question 33
The witness’s testimony provided ______ proof of the suspect’s involvement in the international smuggling ring.
A. thick
B. heavy
C. stiff
D. concrete
Correct answer: D. concrete

Explanation: "Concrete proof" means clear, definite, and convincing evidence that something is true. The witness's testimony provided strong evidence of the suspect's involvement.
Question 34
Her speech about the importance of family really ______ with the audience, moving many to tears.
A. hit the nail
B. struck a chord
C. beat the clock
D. rang a bell
Correct answer: B. struck a chord

Explanation: "Strike a chord with someone" means to create an emotional response or strong feeling in someone. The speech about family deeply affected the audience.
Question 35
Anna: “I didn’t expect the exam to be that difficult.”

Ben: “______”
A. I couldn’t care less. I tried my best.
B. Tell me about it. I barely finished on time.
C. Why are you always complaining?
D. No problem, I can help you next time.
Correct answer: B. Tell me about it. I barely finished on time.

Explanation: "Tell me about it!" is an informal expression used to show that you strongly agree with what someone has just said because you have experienced the same thing. Ben agrees that the exam was difficult because he barely finished it on time.

PART 2 — Word Formation

Question 36
Celine Dion, the world-renowned Canadian singer, has officially announced her return to the stage for a series of concerts in Paris. This news comes after the star has been (VOLUNTARY) ______ absent from the stage since 2022.
Answer: INVOLUNTARILY

Explanation: The meaning is that she was absent from the stage not by her own choice, because of her medical condition. Therefore, the negative adverb involuntarily is required.
Question 37
This condition causes muscle spasms and significant (CONSTRICT) ______ on her vocal cords, which had previously made singing nearly impossible.
Answer: CONSTRICTION

Explanation: After the adjective "significant", we need a noun. Constriction means the act or process of becoming narrower or tighter.
Question 38
Despite these immense physical challenges, Dion has remained (RESILIENCE) ______, expressing her excitement to perform once again.
Answer: RESILIENT

Explanation: After the linking verb "has remained", we need an adjective. Resilient means able to recover quickly from difficulties or challenges.
Question 39
Her recent performance at the Paris Olympics was (TRIUMPH) ______, as critics praised the emotional depth of her voice.
Answer: TRIUMPHANT

Explanation: After the linking verb "was", we need an adjective describing the performance. Triumphant means very successful or victorious.
Question 40
...proving that she has truly “nailed the landing” of her (COME) ______.
Answer: COMEBACK

Explanation: A comeback is a return to a successful career or activity after a period of absence. The context refers to Celine Dion returning to performing after being absent from the stage.
