II. Word Formation

Question 1
Until the twentieth century most scientists argued that dream was nothing but a random jumble of completely (COMPREHEND) images remaining from the sensory accumulation of our daily lives.
Answer: INCOMPREHENSIBLE
Explanation: The meaning is "impossible to understand", so the negative adjective incomprehensible is needed.
Question 2
Since the idea that dreams are meaningful in their own way became popular, psychologists have proposed (COUNT) theories to explain the logic of dreams.
Answer: COUNTLESS
Explanation: "Countless" means too many to be counted, which fits the context.
Question 3
A dream can be a response to events in the outside world, or it can (ORIGIN) within, expressing aspects of the dreamer’s deep-seated feelings.
Answer: ORIGINATE
Explanation: After "can", we need the base form of the verb: originate.
Question 4
The language of dreams can warp time, bringing together historical and contemporary figures. It can mix the familiar with the (KNOW).
Answer: UNKNOWN
Explanation: The opposite of "familiar" is "unknown", meaning not known or recognized.
Question 5
People or animals may fly or inanimate things may move (DEPEND) and talk.
Answer: INDEPENDENTLY
Explanation: We need an adverb modifying the verb "move". The correct form is independently.
