Connectors & Result Clauses

That...that • Such...that • After • Although • Despite • Because

🎯 Learning Objectives

In this lesson, you will learn how to connect ideas and express reason, contrast, time, and result in English.

  • Use so...that and such...that to express results.
  • Use after to describe an earlier action.
  • Distinguish although from despite / in spite of.
  • Use because and because of correctly.
  • Apply common phrasal verbs and collocations in context.
💡 Key idea: Before choosing a connector, identify the relationship between the two ideas: reason, contrast, time, or result.

PART 1. SO...THAT – QUÁ... ĐẾN NỖI MÀ

Cấu trúc so...that dùng để diễn tả một mức độ hoặc kết quả.

S + be + so + adjective + that + clause
S + V + so + adverb + that + clause

🔹 So + adjective + that

The journey was so difficult that we almost gave up.

→ Hành trình khó khăn đến nỗi chúng tôi gần như bỏ cuộc.

The weather was so bad that we had to cancel the trip.

→ Thời tiết tệ đến nỗi chúng tôi phải hủy chuyến đi.

🔹 So + adverb + that

She spoke so quietly that nobody could hear her.

→ Cô ấy nói nhỏ đến nỗi không ai có thể nghe thấy.

📌 SO thường đứng trước adjective hoặc adverb.
2 / 9

PART 2. SUCH...THAT – QUÁ... ĐẾN NỖI MÀ

Such...that cũng diễn tả kết quả, nhưng such thường đứng trước một cụm danh từ.

such + a/an + adjective + singular countable noun + that + clause
such + adjective + plural noun + that + clause
such + adjective + uncountable noun + that + clause

🔹 Such + a/an + adjective + noun

It was such a difficult journey that we almost gave up.

→ Đó là một hành trình khó đến nỗi chúng tôi gần như bỏ cuộc.

🔹 Such + adjective + plural noun

They were such beautiful places that we took hundreds of photos.

🔹 Such + adjective + uncountable noun

We had such bad weather that we couldn't continue our trip.

💡 SO + adjective/adverb
SUCH + noun phrase
SO...THAT SUCH...THAT
so difficult that... such a difficult journey that...
so beautiful that... such beautiful places that...
so expensive that... such an expensive hotel that...

PART 3. AFTER – SAU KHI

After được dùng để nói rằng một hành động xảy ra sau một hành động khác.

After + S + V, S + V
After + V-ing, S + V

🔹 After + clause

After we visited the museum, we went to a local restaurant.

🔹 After + V-ing

After visiting the museum, we went to a local restaurant.

📌 Khi hai mệnh đề có cùng chủ ngữ, có thể rút gọn:

After + S + V → After + V-ing

🔹 Ví dụ trong ngữ cảnh

After finishing the project, we celebrated together.

After we finished the project, we celebrated together.

PART 4. ALTHOUGH / DESPITE – MẶC DÙ

🔹 ALTHOUGH / THOUGH / EVEN THOUGH

Dùng trước một mệnh đề hoàn chỉnh gồm chủ ngữ + động từ.

Although + S + V, S + V

Although the journey was difficult, we managed to reach the top.

🔹 DESPITE / IN SPITE OF

Dùng trước noun / noun phrase / V-ing.

Despite + noun / noun phrase
Despite + V-ing

Despite the difficult journey, we reached the top.

Despite being tired, we continued walking.

❌ Despite the journey was difficult

✅ Although the journey was difficult
✅ Despite the difficult journey

🔹 So sánh

Cấu trúc Ví dụ
Although + S + V Although it was raining, we went out.
Despite + noun Despite the rain, we went out.
Despite + V-ing Despite raining heavily, we went out.

PART 5. BECAUSE / BECAUSE OF – BỞI VÌ

🔹 BECAUSE

Because đứng trước một mệnh đề hoàn chỉnh.

Because + S + V, S + V

We stayed at the hotel because it was raining heavily.

🔹 BECAUSE OF

Because of đứng trước danh từ, cụm danh từ hoặc V-ing.

Because of + noun / noun phrase / V-ing

We stayed at the hotel because of the heavy rain.

The flight was delayed because of bad weather.

Because Because of
because + S + V because of + noun
because it rained because of the rain
because the weather was bad because of bad weather
💡 Mẹo nhớ:

BECAUSE → có S + V
BECAUSE OF → không có mệnh đề hoàn chỉnh

PART 6. PHRASAL VERBS

Dưới đây là một số phrasal verbs cơ bản thường gặp trong chủ đề experiences, travel, study và daily life.

Phrasal Verb Nghĩa Ví dụ
find out tìm ra, phát hiện We found out the truth.
look for tìm kiếm We looked for a cheap hotel.
look after chăm sóc She looks after her younger brother.
set off khởi hành We set off early in the morning.
check in làm thủ tục nhận phòng / lên máy bay We checked in at the hotel.
check out trả phòng We checked out at noon.
give up từ bỏ Don't give up when things get difficult.
carry on tiếp tục We carried on walking despite the rain.
come across tình cờ gặp / bắt gặp We came across an old temple.
take part in tham gia She took part in a volunteer project.

PART 7. USEFUL COLLOCATIONS

Học theo cụm từ giúp bạn sử dụng tiếng Anh tự nhiên và hiệu quả hơn.

Collocation Nghĩa Ví dụ
make a contribution đóng góp Everyone can make a contribution to society.
cut down on cắt giảm We should cut down on plastic waste.
take responsibility for chịu trách nhiệm về We should take responsibility for our actions.
make an effort nỗ lực You should make an effort to improve your English.
have an impact on có ảnh hưởng đến Technology has an impact on our lives.
play a role in đóng vai trò trong Education plays an important role in society.
take advantage of tận dụng Students should take advantage of learning opportunities.
deal with giải quyết / đối phó với We need to deal with this problem quickly.
⭐ Useful combinations:

make a contribution
make an effort
take responsibility for something
have an impact on something
play a role in something
cut down on something

🎯 FINAL SUMMARY

1. RESULT – KẾT QUẢ

so + adjective/adverb + that + clause
such + (a/an) + adjective + noun + that + clause

2. TIME – THỜI GIAN

after + S + V
after + V-ing

3. CONTRAST – TƯƠNG PHẢN

although + S + V
despite/in spite of + noun/V-ing

4. REASON – NGUYÊN NHÂN

because + S + V
because of + noun/V-ing

🔥 Quick Check

Although the weather was bad, we continued our trip.

Despite the bad weather, we continued our trip.

The weather was so bad that we almost cancelled the trip.

It was such bad weather that we almost cancelled the trip.

We stayed inside because it was raining.

We stayed inside because of the rain.

⚠️ Remember:

Although + CLAUSE ✅
Despite + NOUN / V-ING ✅

Because + CLAUSE ✅
Because of + NOUN / V-ING ✅
