PART 10. WRITING CHALLENGE ✍️

An Unforgettable Experience

Write 100–120 words.

Viết về một trải nghiệm đáng nhớ của em. Hãy sử dụng ít nhất:

  • 2 động từ + to-infinitive
  • 2 động từ + V-ing
  • 1 cấu trúc remember + V-ing / remember + to-V
  • 1 cấu trúc stop + to-V / stop + V-ing
  • 1 cấu trúc try + to-V / try + V-ing
  • Mô tả rõ khi nào, ở đâu và chuyện gì đã xảy ra


Sample Answer:

One of the most unforgettable experiences I have ever had was a volunteer trip to a small village last summer. I decided to join the trip because I wanted to help local people. Before leaving home, I remembered to bring some books and clothes for the children. During the trip, I enjoyed meeting the villagers and learning about their culture. We stopped to take photos of the beautiful scenery and tried asking local people about their traditions. Although the journey was tiring, I managed to complete the trip successfully. I will never forget visiting that wonderful village.
