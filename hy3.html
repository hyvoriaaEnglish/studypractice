📝 Questions 76–78: Finish each sentence so that it means exactly the same as the sentence printed before it.
Question 76
I only understood Hamlet after seeing it on the stage.

→ Only after ________________________________________________.
Model answer:
Only after seeing Hamlet on the stage did I understand it.
💡 Explanation: When a sentence begins with "Only after...", we use inversion:

Only after + time expression + did + subject + verb.

Therefore:
Only after seeing Hamlet on the stage did I understand it.
Question 77
The little boy could prepare well for the presentation because his mother supported him.

→ Without his ________________________________________________.
Model answer:
Without his mother's support, the little boy couldn't have prepared well for the presentation .
💡 Explanation: The original sentence describes a past situation that depended on his mother's support. We can rewrite this using:

Without + noun, subject + couldn't have + past participle .

"his mother supported him" becomes "his mother's support".
Question 78
He worked diligently, but his efforts were not recognized by his boss.

→ Diligently as ________________________________________________.
Model answer:
Diligently as he worked, his efforts were not recognized by his boss.
💡 Explanation: Adjective / Adverb + as + subject + verb can be used to express contrast.

"Diligently as he worked" means:
"Although he worked diligently."
