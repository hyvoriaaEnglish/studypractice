Vocabulary & Word Formation Quiz

Part 1: Phrasal Verbs

1. We are encouraging the people in our community to ______ plastic bags.

2. I'm ______ a cold. I have a runny nose and a sore throat.

3. We found a buyer for our house, but then the sale ______.

4. The flowers in my back garden are ______ a fragrant perfume.

5. Olive has ______ more responsibilities, so she has to work overtime to complete the tasks on time.

Part 2: Word Formation

6. Finding a career that brings you ______ is crucial for long-term happiness. (SATISFY)

7. A large international meeting was held with the aim of promoting ______ development in all countries. (SUSTAIN)

8. We find the idea of exploring new places ______, as it brings excitement and joy. (EXHILARATE)

9. Never try to mend a broken machine without ______ it from the electricity supply. (CONNECT)

10. The number 30 on a road sign ______ that the speed limit is 30 miles an hour. (SIGNIFICANT)

11. Hana's scarf was ______ placed to hide a tear in her shirt. (STRATEGY)

Part 3: Advanced Word Formation

12. David Attenborough, a passionate ______, fights tirelessly to keep the forests alive. (PRESERVE)

13. In our modern days, stress has an ______ more serious effect on our lives than we realize. (MEASURE)

14. His life was carefully ______ with his work in one city and his social life in another. (COMPARTMENT)

15. Many of the children were clearly ______ and suffering from various diseases. (NOURISH)
