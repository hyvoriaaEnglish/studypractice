Reading Comprehension: Navigating Economic Challenges

Reading Passage

In recent years, the global economy has faced significant uncertainty. Central banks around the world have had to adjust their monetary policy frequently to combat rising inflation. When consumer prices surge, the purchasing power of everyday citizens declines, making the cost of living a major concern. To slow down rapid price increases, policy makers often raise the interest rate, though this decision can sometimes cool down economic growth and increase the unemployment rate.

For businesses, staying competitive in a volatile market requires careful planning. Companies must manage their assets and liabilities wisely to maintain high liquidity during difficult times. When a country enters a recession, consumer demand drops sharply, causing corporate revenue and profit to shrink. In severe cases, poor financial management or overwhelming debt can lead a firm directly into bankruptcy.

International trade also plays a crucial role in shaping a nation’s GDP. Governments use fiscal policy, including taxes and subsidies, to protect domestic industries. However, when a government imposes high tariffs or other trade barriers on imported goods, it can trigger trade tensions with foreign partners. While protecting local production may secure jobs for a while, it can also disrupt the global supply chain and limit access to key commodities.

Ultimately, achieving market equilibrium is a complex balancing act. Whether dealing with scarcity of raw materials or shifting market trends, understanding these core economic principles helps both policy makers and investors make informed decisions for long-term prosperity.
