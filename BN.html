II. Verb Forms

Instruction: Give the correct tense/form of the verb in brackets to complete each sentence.
1. Though (write) ______ for children, “Alice in the Wonderland” appeals to many adult readers, too.
2. His roommate (always enter) ______ the room without knocking first, which annoys him a lot.
3. The woman insisted that the lost child (take) ______ to the store’s information desk so his parents could be paged.
4. I can’t help (choose) ______ as a school representative; it was the head teacher’s decision, so you needn’t be jealous.
5. ‘Didn’t you know that Olivia works as a curator?’ ‘To be perfectly honest, she (tell) ______ me, but I probably forgot.’

III. Word Formation

Instruction: Give the correct form of the given word to complete each sentence.
1. We should make young people realize that drug-taking is ______ (DESTROY).
2. Lots of smart people are interested in the problems of unhealthy and ______ (PRODUCE) workplaces and what to do about them.
3. Vienna ______ (MEMORY) the 200th anniversary of Schubert’s birth with a series of exhibitions and concerts.
4. The stories ______ (VARY) focus on domestic problems they may have experienced themselves.
5. The principal said some ______ (COURAGE) words to the pupils before the exam.
6. The Manufacturing Technology Show in Chicago attracted nearly 90,000 ______ (ATTEND) and 1,200 exhibitors.
7. The purpose of the United Nations is the ______ (MAINTAIN) of international peace and security.
8. Melanin producing cells in our bodies are ______ (ACTIVE) by ultraviolet light from the sun and can have a protective effect.
9. The heavy rain made the cave ______ (ACCESS). The water level was so high that it would be dangerous to enter the cave.
10. I’m full of ______ (ADMIRE) for Terry – she’s really achieved a lot in her life.
